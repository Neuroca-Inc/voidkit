# VoidKit Math Mining TODO

**Purpose:** single root-level source of truth for turning the VDM Math Mining v3 ore corpus into reusable VoidKit mathematics without losing authored mechanism identity, research value, derivational context, or scientific custody.

This document is **not a deletion queue, normalization queue, or prior-art gate**. The mining corpus is treated as a high-value research/software asset containing ordinary reusable mathematics, distinctive implementations, authored mechanisms, potentially novel methods, and potentially novel mathematical results. The job is to recover and strengthen that value, not to compress it into familiar library shapes.

**Mining snapshot audited:** `VDM_Math_Mining_for_VoidKit_v3(1).zip`.

## Authorial operating mandate

VoidKit work is an **active authoring and engineering collaboration**, not an external review board standing between the research and its own library.

1. **Act as a builder, not a gatekeeper.** When a useful mathematical object is present, the default action is to understand it, preserve it, extract it, test it, document it, and make it usable.
2. **The repository owner's explicit scientific and architectural decisions control the project.** An agent may identify an exact contradiction, defect, unsupported claim, or prior-art concern, but may not substitute its own preferred theory, architecture, naming, or scope.
3. **Do not require novelty proof before the owner's own mathematics may enter VoidKit.** Novelty review is a separate research burden. `NOVELTY UNASSESSED` is not a reason to block extraction, packaging, testing, or use.
4. **Do not assume familiarity implies equivalence.** A solver that resembles a standard method may still contain a distinctive derivation, invariant, numerical strategy, certification method, performance property, composition, or use surface. Compare exact objects before classifying them.
5. **Do not erase authorship by genericization.** Named authored mechanisms and method families remain identifiable unless the owner explicitly authorizes a rename or retirement.
6. **Generalization is additive by default.** If a generic primitive can be factored from an authored method, keep the authored method intact and expose the generic primitive alongside it. Do not replace the authored object with the abstraction.
7. **Preserve the strongest recoverable object first.** Do not simplify away equations, branches, certificates, controls, exact arithmetic, failure semantics, alternate implementations, or derivational structure merely to create a smaller API.
8. **Uncertainty triggers investigation, not disposal.** If an item's significance, novelty, canonical variant, or mathematical role is unclear, keep it in custody and mark the uncertainty explicitly.
9. **Curiosity precedes compression.** Reconstruct what the code is actually doing and why before translating it into conventional terminology or deciding that an existing library already covers it.
10. **Every pass should create value.** Prefer a working extraction, verified equivalence result, recovered derivation, tested invariant, or sharply localized open question over commentary that merely raises barriers.

## Status vocabulary

- **COMPLETED** — the item has a deliberate current disposition in the active repository (implemented or explicitly retired/quarantined).
- **STARTED** — implementation/extraction work exists, but it is incomplete, unmerged, or still needs source reconciliation.
- **PLANNED** — selected/promoted as a target, but no usable current implementation is merged.
- **NOT STARTED** — retained as ore, but no extraction commitment/work has been made yet.

`sources/` and mining archives do **not** count as implementation. A capability only counts as merged when it has a clean public location, tests, and provenance/custody notes.

### Research-value / novelty vocabulary

Implementation status and research significance are separate axes. Where an item has been examined deeply enough, use one of these labels in notes, provenance, or the capability record:

- **KNOWN METHOD** — the mathematical method is established and this implementation does not currently claim a distinct method.
- **DISTINCT IMPLEMENTATION** — established mathematics implemented with a materially distinctive algorithm, architecture, numerical strategy, certification path, performance property, or composition.
- **DERIVED VARIANT** — a nontrivial variant derived from known mathematics with its own stated assumptions or construction.
- **POTENTIALLY NOVEL METHOD** — the implementation/derivation may constitute a new method; novelty has not yet been established against prior art.
- **POTENTIALLY NOVEL RESULT** — the underlying mathematical result may itself be new; novelty has not yet been established against prior art.
- **NOVELTY UNASSESSED** — no responsible novelty classification has yet been performed.

These labels are **not quality rankings**. A known method can still be an excellent VoidKit tool. A potentially novel item does not become a novelty claim until the proper prior-art and evidence burden is completed.

**Critical rule:** novelty assessment is never a prerequisite for preserving, extracting, testing, or using the owner's own mathematics. When novelty matters for publication or priority, perform a dedicated literature/prior-art comparison without weakening or withholding the implementation in the meantime.

## Non-negotiable custody rules

1. **Mine for capability, not familiarity.** Do not mirror the VDM research directory tree, but also do not force every item into conventional library categories before its mathematical identity is understood.
2. **Portable does not mean generic.** Authored mechanisms such as **SIE** remain themselves even when reusable outside VDM. SIE is the authored Self-Improvement Engine multi-objective reward function; it must not be renamed into a generic signal utility.
3. **Namespace follows mathematical ownership.** VDM-specific mathematics stays under `voidkit/vdm/`; Phase Calculus stays under `voidkit/phase_calculus/`; genuinely independent primitives may live elsewhere. Origin alone does not make a primitive VDM- or Phase-specific.
4. **No destructive genericization.** A generic reusable primitive may be extracted from an authored method only additively. The original authored method, equations, name, provenance, and scope remain available unless explicitly retired.
5. **No novelty gatekeeping.** Never block an extraction because a literature search has not yet established novelty. Record `NOVELTY UNASSESSED` or a potential-novelty status and continue the engineering work.
6. **No equivalence by resemblance.** Do not discard, replace, or demote an implementation merely because NumPy, SciPy, SymPy, a textbook, or another library has something with a similar name. Establish mathematical/API equivalence and compare the actual algorithmic or evidentiary contribution first.
7. **Preserve potentially load-bearing details.** Exact arithmetic, branch handling, invariants, certificates, convergence gates, negative controls, sparse/locality properties, alternate formulations, and performance-relevant mechanisms are part of the object until shown otherwise.
8. **Never silently replace a research equation with a cleaner-looking one.** Normalize names/APIs only when the mathematical contract is preserved and the change is recorded. A changed equation is a successor method, not cleanup.
9. **Alternate implementations are evidence.** Python/C/Rust/assembly/SymPy variants may encode independent derivations, optimized realizations, regression oracles, ABI surfaces, or cross-checks. Select a canonical public API without throwing the others away.
10. **Canonicalization requires reconciliation.** When multiple generations exist, identify which is authoritative, which are ancestors, which are controls, and what changed. Do not pick the newest filename automatically.
11. **Research harnesses can contain ore.** Do not promote whole experiment/report harnesses as public APIs, but inspect them for reusable kernels, certifiers, diagnostics, estimators, controls, and algorithms before excluding them.
12. **Tests must protect source semantics, not merely the rewrite.** Wherever feasible, compare extracted behavior to frozen source receipts, exact identities, analytical solutions, cross-language implementations, or independently derived controls.
13. **Do not erase negative or bounded results.** A failed branch, finite-domain result, approximation, restricted theorem surface, or unresolved capability boundary may itself carry useful mathematics. Preserve the exact scope.
14. **Do not confuse packaging with scientific correction.** API cleanup, module relocation, typing, vectorization, and dependency cleanup do not authorize changes to mathematical content.
15. **Potential novelty gets extra custody, not extra obstruction.** Preserve derivation lineage and evidence aggressively, expose the implementation for use, and schedule novelty research separately when worthwhile.
16. **When classification is uncertain, retain first.** The safe default for valuable unexplained mathematics is custody plus investigation, not deletion, renaming, or replacement.

## Extraction decision protocol

Before a nontrivial ore item is renamed, generalized, replaced, retired, or declared redundant, record enough information to answer:

1. **Mathematical object:** What equation, operator, transformation, invariant, solver, estimator, proof procedure, state machine, or exact construction is actually implemented?
2. **Assumptions and domain:** Under what hypotheses, branch choices, precision model, topology, discretization, or state semantics is it valid?
3. **Derivational lineage:** Is there an accompanying derivation, paper, proof, symbolic audit, numerical certificate, experiment family, or earlier/later implementation?
4. **Implementation distinction:** What is algorithmically distinctive about this realization, if anything?
5. **Evidence:** What has been proved, symbolically checked, numerically validated, benchmarked, falsified, or left unresolved?
6. **Portability:** Which pieces require VDM or Phase Calculus semantics, and which survive independently?
7. **Research-value status:** Is it a known method, distinct implementation, derived variant, potentially novel method/result, or still novelty-unassessed?
8. **Destination:** Where should the canonical public API live, and what authored wrapper/facade must remain?
9. **Verification:** What independent test can falsify a bad extraction?
10. **Custody:** Which exact source bytes and hashes must remain recoverable?

If these questions are not yet answerable, the item is **not ready for destructive cleanup**. It may still be extracted behind a faithful compatibility or research API while investigation continues.

### Concrete identity examples

- **SIE:** preserve as the authored Self-Improvement Engine multi-objective reward function. Generic sub-operations may be factored only additively.
- **Phase-native π spigot/streamer:** preserve as a method family with generation, streaming, certification, and certified-bank query surfaces. Do not collapse it to “computes π.”
- **Logistic work:** preserve the exact finite-time update, logarithmic invariant, and validation/certification context rather than reducing the contribution to “uses a standard logistic ODE.”
- **Phase Calculus / Orthad / QBL:** establish canonical research authority before flattening historical generations into convenience APIs. General exact-arithmetic primitives may be extracted separately without dissolving the Phase-specific construction that uses them.

## Completion gate for a TODO item

An item moves to **COMPLETED** only when all applicable conditions are true:

- canonical public namespace/API chosen;
- mathematical assumptions and scope documented;
- source path + SHA-256 provenance recorded;
- duplicate/superseded variants reconciled;
- focused correctness tests added, including invariants/negative controls when the source supports them;
- no VDM/Phase-specific semantics were erased merely to make a function look generic;
- authored mechanism/method identity is preserved when the object is not genuinely generic;
- research-value/novelty status is recorded when assessed, or explicitly left `NOVELTY UNASSESSED` without blocking completion;
- potentially distinctive or novel behavior has not been discarded solely because a superficially similar standard method exists;
- import/compile/package tests pass in the active repository;
- README/API index updated so the tool is discoverable.

**Maintenance note:** when a new mining archive is added, append its retained sources to the ledger first, then classify them. Do not let new ore bypass the inventory. Classification must preserve uncertainty honestly: an unfamiliar or apparently redundant item remains in custody until its mathematical object and relationship to existing implementations are actually established.
